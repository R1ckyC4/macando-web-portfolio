What is Project Sentinel?

Work in progress — updated as projects are completed.

During my time as a research intern at WiMNeT I became deeply interested in sensing systems and surveillance, and I wanted my personal engineering projects to reflect that.

Project Sentinel is an umbrella initiative encompassing several interconnected engineering projects. I envision a collection of sensing and actuation nodes tied together into a single IoT-style framework. The core of this system is Sentinel Core, a voice-controlled AI interface inspired by JARVIS and Anduril's LatticeOS, designed to interface with all Sentinel nodes such as robotic arms, radars, cameras, and more through a unified control layer.
