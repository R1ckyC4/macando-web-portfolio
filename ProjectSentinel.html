What is Project Sentinel?

This is work in progress. Over the next months or few years I will fill this in as projects are completed.

During my time as a research intern at WiMNeT I got interested into sensing systems and surveilance, and I wanted some of my engineering projects to reflect that interest.

Project Sentinel is a umbrella term that will emcompass several engineering projects. I envision a collection of sensing and "doing" parts that are all interconnected in one system like a IoT framework. The core of this inititive is SentinelCore, which is heavily inspired by Jarvis from Iron Man and LatticeOS from Anduril Industries. This attempt to recreate Jarvis will be able to access all the nodes of Project Sentinel , which can include robotic arms, radars, cameras, etc, and allow me to control the entire system by voice.
